Start with NexCore Labs

NexCore Labs is an independent, student-led platform built to help the SQU community access useful tools, share work, collaborate, and move ideas forward.

  • Use the Project Hub to discover public student work and shared resources.
  • Use Initiatives to explore NexCore-built products, experiments, and community efforts.
  • Use Contributor Center to share projects, suggest improvements, or follow contribution paths.

Sign in and access rules

Sign in with your Google account. Eligible SQU accounts and pre-approved users can access NexCore under the current access rules, while paid service orders remain temporarily paused.

If access does not appear after signing in, check that you are using the intended Google account, refresh the page, then contact NexCore with the account email and the page you were trying to open.

Use the Project Hub

The Project Hub is the public discovery layer for student work, shared resources, and community projects. It is designed for browsing, comparison, and practical project visibility rather than private file storage.

For new project visibility or corrections to an existing entry, start from the Contributor Center so the request keeps the right context.

Understand initiatives

Initiatives are NexCore products, experiments, and community efforts beyond individual project pages. The page can include launched work, active builds, and ideas still taking shape.

Featured public initiatives may also appear in the site menu as shortcuts so visitors can jump into active work faster.

Pricing and billing

Paid service orders are temporarily paused while NexCore completes legal and regulatory review. PayPal checkout, bank transfer, mobile transfer, and paid order submission are unavailable.

Please do not send payment using old details or links. For the formal policy, read the Pricing & Billing Policy.

Privacy and security basics

NexCore keeps privacy guidance in plain-language public pages. For details about account data, cookies, analytics, and project information, review the Privacy Policy and Terms.

Troubleshooting first steps

  1. Refresh the page and confirm your internet connection.
  2. Sign out and back in if the issue is account-related.
  3. Open the same page in a fresh browser tab if content looks stale.
  4. Contact NexCore with the page URL, account email if relevant, and a short description of what happened.

Still need help?

Send the page link, account context if relevant, and what you expected to happen. Clear reports are faster to handle.
