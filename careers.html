Marketing & outreach

Marketing Coordinator

Help students discover NexCore through useful campaigns, community partnerships, and clear communication.

Volunteer student team roleFlexible, part-time

Communication · Campaign planning · Teamwork

Role details

Responsibilities

  • Plan small awareness campaigns around useful NexCore tools.
  • Gather student feedback and suggest outreach opportunities.
  • Coordinate campaign messages and review what worked.

What we are looking for

  • Clear communication and an interest in student communities.
  • Reliability, initiative, and willingness to learn.

Nice to have

Experience with student activities, campaign planning, or basic design.

Expected commitment

A regular weekly contribution, with hours agreed together around your studies before you join. Tell us your availability in the application.

What you can gain

Hands-on experience, work you can discuss in your portfolio, and collaboration with a student-led team building useful products. This is an unpaid volunteer opportunity.

Content & community

Social Media Coordinator

Shape NexCore’s social presence with helpful content, release announcements, and thoughtful community engagement.

Volunteer student team roleFlexible, part-time

Writing · Content planning · Community engagement

Role details

Responsibilities

  • Plan and prepare posts for NexCore’s social channels.
  • Explain new releases in clear, student-friendly language.
  • Engage respectfully with the community and bring feedback to the team.

What we are looking for

  • An eye for clear writing and useful visual content.
  • Consistency, sound judgment, and collaborative working habits.

Nice to have

Experience creating short videos, using design tools, or managing a student social account.

Expected commitment

A regular weekly contribution, with hours agreed together around your studies before you join. Tell us your availability in the application.

What you can gain

Hands-on experience, work you can discuss in your portfolio, and collaboration with a student-led team building useful products. This is an unpaid volunteer opportunity.
